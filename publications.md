<section class="page-intro">
  <p class="eyebrow">Research</p>
  <h1>Mathematical ideas, made computational.</h1>
  <p class="lede">My interests focus on numerical techniques for approximation and partial differential equations, with particular curiosity about methods that remain flexible beyond traditional meshes.</p>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="research-detail">
    <article>
      <p class="section-number">01</p>
      <h2>Numerical approximation</h2>
      <p>I am interested in how functions and complex mathematical objects can be represented accurately with tractable computational models.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <p class="section-number">02</p>
      <h2>Meshless methods</h2>
      <p>Meshless approaches offer adaptable ways to discretize problems with complicated geometry or evolving domains. I am interested in their mathematical foundations and numerical behavior.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <p class="section-number">03</p>
      <h2>Numerical solutions of PDEs</h2>
      <p>Partial differential equations connect mathematical models to physical and scientific phenomena. My focus is on numerical approaches that balance accuracy, stability, and efficiency.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
